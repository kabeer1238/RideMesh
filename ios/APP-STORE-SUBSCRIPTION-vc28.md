## Review notes suggestion
RideMesh Premium unlocks the app’s ride communication features. To test, open RideMesh and tap Create Ride or Join Ride. If the Apple Account is not currently subscribed, the app presents the RideMesh Premium screen. The subscription uses StoreKit 2 product ID `com.autopilotindia.ridemesh.monthly`. Eligible new subscribers are shown the introductory free trial configured in App Store Connect. The displayed price is loaded dynamically from StoreKit. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall, and subscription management is available from Settings.
